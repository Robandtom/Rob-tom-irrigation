Above-Ground Irrigation

A practical way to automate watering using dripline and micro-irrigation positioned around planting areas.

Why choose above-ground?

These systems are generally simpler to install and easier to inspect, alter or extend as the garden changes.

  • Lower installation disruption
  • Easy inspection and alteration
  • Ideal for borders, shrubs and beds
  • Targeted water delivery
  • Can be fully automated

Good for evolving gardens

Accessible pipework makes future additions and changes straightforward.

Neat routing

Pipe can be carefully positioned around beds and becomes less noticeable as planting establishes.

Flower Beds

Dripline through planting areas for regular watering.

Shrubs & Hedges

Long runs can serve established planting lines.

Trees & Pots

Targeted emitters can serve individual plants.
