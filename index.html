A SYSTEM DESIGNED AROUND YOUR GARDEN

Modern irrigation without the daily watering routine

Our irrigation systems include above-ground drip irrigation, underground drip irrigation, micro-irrigation and pop-up lawn sprinklers. These systems can be used separately or combined in any configuration to suit your garden. We use high-quality components, ensure neat installations and offer smart control options. With every irrigation system we install, all non-drip water supply pipes crossing pathways and other walking areas are discreetly buried beneath them.

Restrictions and exemptions vary between water companies. Always check the current rules issued by your local supplier.

ABOUT ROB & TOM

8 years of experience in garden irrigation.

We are Rob & Tom, providing professional garden irrigation installations across London, Berkshire, Surrey and Buckinghamshire. We have been in the garden irrigation business for 8 years, designing and installing watering systems for gardens of different sizes and layouts.

We design each system around the individual garden rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach. From discreet dripline for flower beds, shrubs and hedges to underground pipework, automated timers and smart watering controls, we aim to make garden watering simple, reliable and efficient.

We take particular care with neat installation and practical pipe routing, keeping visible pipework to a minimum wherever possible.
